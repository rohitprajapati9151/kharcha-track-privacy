Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 30, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Kharcha Track respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Kharcha Track app may collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. Information We Collect

Kharcha Track may process the following information depending on the features you use.

Account Information

When you sign in, we may process:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Firebase user ID
  • Authentication-related information

Kharcha Track currently supports:

  • Google Sign-In
  • Email and password login

Expense Information

When you add expenses, the information you enter may include:

  • Amount
  • Category
  • Date
  • Notes or description
  • Other expense-related information you choose to enter

This information is used to provide the expense tracking functionality of the app.

Local Information

If you use Kharcha Track offline, some app and expense information may be stored locally on your device so that the app can continue working without an internet connection.

Security PIN

If you enable the app security PIN, security-related information is stored using appropriate Android security mechanisms to help protect access to the app.

Crash and Diagnostic Information

Kharcha Track uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix app crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may process information such as:

  • Crash reports
  • Stack traces
  • App version
  • Device and operating system information
  • Technical and diagnostic information

This information is used to improve app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain Kharcha Track
  • Authenticate users
  • Store and synchronize expense information
  • Provide offline functionality
  • Protect the app with the security PIN
  • Find and fix crashes and technical problems
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Respond to support requests
  • Meet applicable legal requirements

We do not use your expense information for advertising purposes.

3. Firebase Services

Kharcha Track uses Firebase services provided by Google:

Firebase Authentication

Used for Google Sign-In and email-based authentication.

Cloud Firestore

Used to store and synchronize applicable account and expense information.

Firebase Crashlytics

Used to identify and diagnose app crashes and technical issues.

These services may process information according to Google's applicable privacy policies and terms.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed by Firebase services when required to provide app functionality.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or a valid legal request.

5. Advertising

Kharcha Track does not currently use Google AdMob or other advertising services. The app currently does not display advertisements.

6. Features Coming Soon

The following features are currently shown as planned or upcoming features and are not currently functional:

  • CSV Import
  • Image Export
  • Receipt or Bill Image Upload
  • AI Receipt Scanning

Because these features are not currently available, Kharcha Track does not currently process user data for these features.

If these features are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Data Storage and Security

Depending on how you use the app, information may be stored:

  • Locally on your device
  • In Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • By Firebase services used for authentication and crash reporting

We use reasonable security measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app's functionality, maintain applicable account and expense information, resolve technical issues, and comply with legal requirements.

Information stored locally on your device may remain until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.
